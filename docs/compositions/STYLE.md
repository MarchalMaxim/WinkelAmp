# Scene style: which theme combos work

A theme is a ~10 s stack of 3–5 widgets, each tied to its own pick set (a strand of the music). These are the user's
verdicts from 2026-11-06, on the Rachmaninoff concerto and on Chopin's Nocturne Op. 48/1, first half (a) and second
half (b). Add one line per new verdict, and keep this page short.

## Rules

- **One family.** The widgets of a theme share one visual language, and each one's character fits its strand's pace and
  feel. A pile of good widgets on separate strands is not a theme.
- **Every note gets its mark,** so the set must be the notes one hears as that strand. Where a melody shares its
  register with chords, take the held, voiced-out notes (at least 0.28 s, the loudest of their chord), not the skyline.
- **Ivory:** keep the rows short, one per phrase, each ending as the next begins; or huddle them with a 0.6-wide region.
- **Fireworks:** only on real ff blows, not on routine beat chords.
- **The current:** a lone light for sad or solo entrances. It must not die off.
- **Pluck:** for quick grace notes and trills (the user, 2026-11-10).
- **Handover:** milky white (its default now), and only on long, slow notes. On a fast line it flashes.
- **Snap squares:** small and calm, in their own red (not milky).
- **Fields:** not under every scene. On black is fine.
- **Slow, quiet passages:** no busy red textures.
- **Flee:** only on masses of unorganised notes, never on picked notes.
- **The screen:** full (at least four threads), except at deliberate, spare entrances.

## Rejected: don't repeat

- **Concerto:** Tack ("gluey"); Mica on a soft left hand; film, fist (Fist of spines, once urchin) and mica on a horn call; an Ivory ladder that
  misses notes; a crowded coda (interlock, Ivory, fireworks and squares); snap squares in an agitato.
- **Nocturne a:** fireworks on the beat chords under Ivory; crimp's red mesh on a slow chorale.
- **Nocturne b:**
  - handover on a fast agitato theme (flashing);
  - Ivory on a skyline melody inside repeated chords (off the notes);
  - hammer, mica and squares on pp final chords ("very incongruent");
  - the second half of the ff climax.
