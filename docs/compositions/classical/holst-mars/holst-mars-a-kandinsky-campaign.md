# Holst: Mars, the Bringer of War · A · Kandinsky campaign

Show `holst-mars-a-kandinsky-campaign` (open `/?show=holst-mars-a-kandinsky-campaign`). Built by
`build-holst-mars-a-kandinsky-campaign.mjs` in this folder, from `picks.json`.

- **Concept:** Kandinsky's plane is the army on the march. Every note paints: heavy black bars low, blue circles in the middle, yellow triangles high. Dread squares hammer the 5/4 ostinato and brass gust wedges burst on the fanfares. At the dissonant climaxes the casket melts the painting and pixel sort pulls the melt into sheets.
- **Keywords:** brass onslaught, marching army, menace, dissonant climax.
- **Palette:** Kandinsky's primaries and black bars over dread red, on gust's aubergine floor. At the climaxes it tears into blue, gold and red melt sheets. One grey fog in the middle, and pure black at the end.

## What to judge

- **Kandinsky + casket + pixel sort** (untested): does the full Kandinsky plane melt and tear well at the climaxes (1:20, 6:00)?
- **Gust** (its first show) as the brass: wedges on the fanfares, one full blast alone after the general pause (3:06).
- **Kandinsky at 24% transcription coverage:** where the roll is thin, its picks come from the audio.
- **Kandinsky on a Sugimoto horizon** (new, cross-family) for the tenor tuba.
- **The arc:** the army approaches in a growing window, takes over the screen, melts, goes black for the pause, then comes back and burns. At the end, squares alone.

Serendipity:
- **The approach.** At the start the squares are a small copy of themselves, far off on the horizon, and the window grows at every turn of the opening until the army fills the screen (the composer's idea).
- **The eye of the storm.** The tenor tuba plays almost only notes below Kandinsky's bar split, so its notes become black bars, and black bars vanish on a dark floor. As the drone after the Eb blow dies, a grey Sugimoto fog rises under them. It is the only light ground in the show, so the tuba paints black weights on a pale plane, as Kandinsky did. The fog lifts as the trumpet calls come in.
- **The burnt plane.** After the pile-up, the casket keeps melting for 28 s while the grinding held chords fall in volume. Their big red circles, rings and checker grid slide and drip, over the smoke of the brass.
- **The army's last figure.** Kandinsky comes back for the last chord only, at full paint, in red. Its circles hold through the six-second ff chord, melt under the casket, and are cut with the orchestra.
