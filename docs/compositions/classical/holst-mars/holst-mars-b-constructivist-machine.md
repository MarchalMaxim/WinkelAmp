# Holst: Mars, the Bringer of War. B: Constructivist machine

Show: `holst-mars-b-constructivist-machine` (open as `/?show=holst-mars-b-constructivist-machine`). Build:
`build-holst-mars-b-constructivist-machine.mjs` in this folder.

- **Concept:** war as a hard-edged machine. Suprematist planes drift in the quiet, then pile up and tilt in the brass.
  Stella's frames step in on every stroke of the 5/4 ostinato, so the rhythm stacks up as stripes. McLaren's film cards
  stream down a diagonal band through the slow middle. Cut stands a hard slab on each brass blow, and every later blow
  chips it away.
- **Keywords:** relentless 5/4, war machine, mechanised warfare, pounding ostinato.
- **Palette:** Malevich red, ochre and cream planes on a dark field. Cold steel and violet through the slow middle, hot
  gold at the pile-up. Stella's bone frames turn the colours under them negative. Pale sand slabs.

## What to judge

- **Four widgets in a combo that has not been tried before:** suprematism (the ground, all notes), stella (the
  ostinato), cut (the brass blows) and mclaren (the march). None of them has been tested on an orchestra yet.
- **Stella on top of suprematism.** Stella turns the picture under it negative, so in the return (4:17) its thick bands
  turn the red and black planes into grey and bone armour plates.
- **A black-ground widget in a see-through window:** mclaren in a tilted band 0.42 wide, with clear blend, over the
  planes. Its colours come from the harmony (lime, magenta, cyan). There is no knob that makes them red, so you need to
  judge whether this break from the palette works.
- **Stella in windows, the "approaching machine" (new):** the ostinato starts far away, as a small tunnel of frames
  in the centre. It moves nearer at turns of the music, then fills the frame. The same story comes back, compressed,
  in the hush and run-up before the end.
- **Cut's slabs:** sand-coloured blocks on the blows, which crumble to rubble.

**Serendipity:** the machine approaching. Stella is a full-frame widget, but here it is cast as a distant object: a box
0.22 wide (0:08), then 0.5 wide on the cellos' first G stroke (0:20.6), then full frame on the forte climb (0:49.2). In
the hush (6:26.9) it shrinks back to the far box, the run-up's scale (6:33.2) brings it near in one breath, and the
pesante blows land full frame. The composer had two finds of his own:
- the negative stella armour over red planes in the return;
- a second, small stella in the centre for the chord blocks, so you get frames inside the frames.

The general pause (3:05.6) is total black, and the ff blow then lands as slabs on black.

**Still weak:**
- 0:21-0:49 has only two threads. It is meant as a deliberate approach.
- 2:12-2:45 is sparse: only 26 call notes on the band.
- Cut's crumbs still look like soft beige clumps for 2-3 s after big blows.
- McLaren's neon colours are the widget's own, and no knob changes them.
