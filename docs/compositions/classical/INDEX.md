# Classical, 2026-11-06

36 WinkelAmp shows on 9 pieces, plus one built from chapters (folder 10), made to judge widget themes and combinations. 720p, 30 fps, with sound.

**How to watch.** Each video has a legend (.md) beside it. The legend gives the concept and the widgets per scene, and what to judge. Its **Serendipity** line names the surprises the composer tried; judge those especially.

**The letters.**
- **A:** a proven family pushed further.
- **B:** a new family.
- **C:** one radical idea, often a single widget carrying the whole piece.

Romeo and Juliet is cut into 6 parts, each with two cycles:
- **A, Milk and thorn:** single marks on black, the nature widgets as leitmotifs.
- **B, Ink and wash:** continuous ink and painted strokes.

To open a show in the studio, use `/?show=<id>` on branch `claude/classical-widget-themes-3fc81a`.

## 01 Liszt - Hungarian Rhapsody No 2

### A · Bandleader's octaves (10:31)

- **File:** Hungarian Rhapsody 2 - A Bandleader's octaves - 720p.mp4
- **Show:** `liszt-hungarian-2-a-bandleaders-octaves`
- **Widgets:** interlock, hammer, pluck, ivory, gliss, fireworks-snap, fields
- **Watch for:** Watch the solid oxblood wedges cut into the lit field, and the lassan's ff (1:24-1:32.5) and climax (4:06-4:11.5), each blazing at full the instant before its hard cut to black. Also the dip's whisper in octaves (8:36), Gliss's bell on cadenza I (1:33), and Ivory and fireworks held back until the friska. Judge whether the slow Lento still feels too sparse.

### B · Hot plate (10:31)

- **File:** Hungarian Rhapsody 2 - B Hot plate - 720p.mp4
- **Show:** `liszt-hungarian-2-b-hot-plate`
- **Widgets:** perlin, leidenfrost, flee, fireworks
- **Watch for:** Judge Perlin and Leidenfrost as a pair: a molten plate whose sea level and colour tell the temperature (amber, pearl, indigo, amber, white-hot at the last blow), with glass drops as leitmotifs, blue-flame fireworks on the ff tuttis, and Flee steam over a visibly boiling prestissimo plate.

### C · Optical poem (10:31)

- **File:** Hungarian Rhapsody 2 - C Optical poem - 720p.mp4
- **Show:** `liszt-hungarian-2-c-optical-poem`
- **Widgets:** fischinger
- **Watch for:** Fischinger alone: watch the slow lassan as one blue panel of three layered copies (a new trick: lighten-blended full-screen instances shifted by register), break into a window-to-window chase at the friska, and fold back into one panel for the stretta.

## 10 Chapters - Saint-Saens Danse Macabre

The first show built from chapters: each theme is a visual room that leaves and comes back the same, and the developments are made from the rooms you already know.

### Six Rooms at Midnight (7:10)

- **File:** Danse Macabre - Chapters - Six Rooms at Midnight - 720p.mp4
- **Show:** `danse-macabre-d-chapters`
- **Watch for:** Six visual rooms (grey sea, oxblood velvet, Delaunay ballroom, indigo sigh, pale chapel, cyan X-ray stripes) that leave and come back identical. Watch the developments cut them into staves, moons, a frieze, strips and a four-room grid.

The six worlds:
- **M, Midnight**: grey photographic sea, amber bell pillars, pizzicato threads (spare)
- **D, Death's fiddle**: oxblood velvet bands and red thorns on black
- **A, the Waltz**: multicoloured Delaunay ring discs on black
- **B, the Sigh**: indigo-violet field, gold thread, woven band, huddled ivory keys
- **C, the Chorale**: pale ruled Martin page, LeWitt pencil rays, Stella frames (the only light room)
- **X, the Bones**: black and cold-cyan Riley stripes, an X-ray, first glimpsed through a keyhole at the upper left
