## How this was made
- `node docs/compositions/classical/firebird-berceuse-finale/gen-picks.mjs` writes `picks.json` and prints one line per set: notes, attacks, span, velocity range, source and first pitches. It throws on a non-slug id, unsorted notes, a bad note, or a note past the end.
- **The roll** is `pack.tracks`, which the picker shows for this pack. Velocities come mostly from loudness. Where YourMT3+ wrote the same note on violin, viola and cello at once, a set keeps one copy.
- **Audio notes** are used where the transcription has none. They were measured with librosa and are frozen in the generator as AUDIO.
  - harp-rocking: the strongest pitch in the Ab3-C4 band, snapped to that band's onsets.
  - horn-hymn: the solo segmented from the CQT, G3-Bb4.
  - The strokes (7/4, Maestoso, coda, final, harp bridge, last harp notes): audio onsets. Where the roll has a chord within 60 ms, its lowest and highest notes are used; otherwise the stroke's strongest pitch class, as an octave.
  - Velocity = clamp(122 + 3 x dB, 20, 122), dB relative to the loudest moment (0 dB -> 122, -10 -> 92, -20 -> 62, -30 -> 32).
  - Audio notes are not on the picker's roll. The picker shows them as picks it cannot find, but they play.
- **Trust.** Onset coverage is 34 %. YourMT3+ hears the strings well. It splits the one bassoon across bassoon, clarinet, horn and voice, and barely hears the horn solo, the 7/4 entry, the coda and the final chord. The `voice` track (19 notes) is not a singer: in the Berceuse it holds oboe-like answers. The `piano` track is the harp and celesta (and the keyboard colour of the tutti).
