# Firebird, Berceuse and Finale: A, "Lantern to hymn"

**Piece:** Stravinsky, *The Firebird*: the Berceuse (lullaby) and the Finale.
**Concept:** STYLE's "lone light, then keys", stretched over the whole spell. The bassoon's lullaby is a lantern: one white-gold line. The harp's rocking is a ring that turns for three minutes. The horn's hymn rises as huddled Ivory keys. The ring then opens into the arch of the sunrise. The lantern comes back as the Firebird's river of gold in the 7/4.
**Keywords:** lullaby spell, hypnotic bassoon, noble horn melody, triumphant sunrise.
**Palette:** a white-gold line and an ice-violet ring over dim dusk fields. Then bone-white keys, ember threads and gold fire on black. Then amber.
**Show:** `shows/firebird-berceuse-finale-a-lantern-to-hymn.json`. Build: `build-firebird-berceuse-finale-a-lantern-to-hymn.mjs` (this folder).

## What to judge

- **Comfort as the ground under a sparsely transcribed orchestra (untested).** Only 34% of the notes were transcribed. Comfort gets every note, plus picks taken from the audio where notes are missing, so the near-silent spell never goes black. Comfort's warmth is set per note and tells the story: dusk, coolest in the spell, then the sunrise to amber.
- **Woven threads closed into a ring (bend 1) for the hypnotic rocking (new).** Later, the same widget opens note by note from ring to arch as the hymn grows.
- **Ivory on a horn hymn (untested):** huddled, strike 0, so the keys swell like a horn. Each statement stands higher and larger than the last.
- **Ivory with woven threads together (untested):** the hymn statements from 4:12 to 5:19.
- **The current, three ways:** a lone lantern, a gold river on black, and a reborn braid on the last chord.
- **Fireworks-snap on the 7/4 and Maestoso bar blows only:** small on the bar starts, big on the four real ff blows.

**Serendipity:**
- **The cradle as a closed ring** (woven threads at bend 1, against the widget's arch default). A spell is a circle. The same widget then opens from ring to arch through the hymn (bend set per note, 0.9 -> 0.32), so the lullaby's circle becomes the sunrise's arch. This was the critic's idea.
- **The 7/4 on pure black:** the sun (Comfort) is withdrawn while the river and the brass fire burn, and it returns on the Maestoso's first ff blow. This was the critic's idea.
- **The composer's ideas:**
  - the lantern reborn as the river on the first 7/4 blow;
  - the Ivory statements climbing the screen like the sun;
  - the final keys pressed back into the ground by the last stroke;
  - Comfort's warmth and breath set per note across the whole show.

**Still weak:**
- The Berceuse is three strands (ring, line, fields) for three minutes. It is spare, but below the "four threads" taste.
- The lantern's line is jagged rather than smooth.
- In the 7/4, the river leaves a short straight line once, where it touches the top edge.
- The smoke of the first 7/4 blow is a dull tan.
- The Ivory rows in the crescendo and Maestoso are short (2-4 keys).
