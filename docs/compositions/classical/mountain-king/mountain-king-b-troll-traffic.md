# Troll traffic: legend

**Piece:** Grieg, *In the Hall of the Mountain King* (Karajan, Berlin Philharmonic). Show `mountain-king-b-troll-traffic`.

**Concept:** the round-9 characters on Mondrian's *Broadway Boogie Woogie*. Boogie's lanes are the city of the trolls and
hear every note. Clumsy's mismatched shapes, in hard-edged mustard planes, are the crowds that carry the tune. Royal's
red brushstroke stands in a tall dark door as the king and ignores his court. Brass gust blows through the frenzy.

**Keywords:** trolls, mischievous, tiptoeing bassoons, chase.

**Palette:** cream and yellow lanes with red, blue and grey blocks; mustard planes of mismatched clumsy shapes; one
oxblood stroke in a black door; orange brass gusts. The city goes from day to night on the frenzy's downbeat.

## What to judge

- **Clumsy, Royal and Gust**, none of them used in a show before. Clumsy runs as three small copies at once (bassoons,
  strings, cymbals), each one a Mondrian plane.
- **Boogie woogie (rated LOVE) as the ground under character widgets.** It is drawn three times: a 6-lane town, a
  10-lane street and a 13-lane hall at night.
- **Royal's flicks are timed to the form.** The court's velocities are shaped so the king rises and flicks exactly three
  times: on the frenzy's downbeat, on the turn after the break, and on the last loud stroke.
- **Gust over a window.** In the coda the brass is drawn over the king's door and breaks the three-column frame apart.

**Serendipity:**
- Mondrian's coloured planes are made of Clumsy small copies, and the king stands in a door: a window, cropped to the
  middle third of his picture.
- The king's three flicks are composed by shaping the court's velocities, and each flick reaches the whole city.
- Each city is lit underneath the one before it, so the cuts land in moving traffic.
- The only time the music stands still, the traffic stands still too.
- The coda's brass breaks out over the king's door. It is the one time anything crosses him, and he still ignores it.

**Known weak spots:**
- The "jam" is sparser than Mondrian. Boogie drops one block per crossing.
- The king is very still for the 50 s of statement 1. That is aloofness by design, but it may read as inert.
- The planes have empty mustard above their crowds.
