# Mountain King A: Threads and dread

**Piece:** Grieg, *In the Hall of the Mountain King* (Karajan, Berlin Philharmonic), 2:30 of music, then silence.
**Concept:** woven threads and dread squares, run as one accelerating machine. The tune climbs out of a cave, storey by
storey, until it fills the hall. Then the frenzy tears the picture apart, the break freezes it, and one casket slash ends it.
**Keywords:** tiptoeing bassoons, pizzicato footsteps, accelerando, frenzy.
**Palette:** ember threads and dread red on black, with white-hot flee streaks.
**Open:** `/?show=mountain-king-a-threads-and-dread` · build: `build-mountain-king-a-threads-and-dread.mjs` (this folder).

## What to judge

- **Squares + strings + blocks (untested).** Do the glitch blocks tearing the threads and squares into sliding slabs read
  as one family? Judge it at 1:42 (the frenzy's downbeat) and 2:11 (the break).
- **The dread family at a fast tempo.** Squares and strings were loved on slow music. Do they hold up through the frenzy?
- **The accelerando as a tightening weave (new).** The tune's threads add one crossing per section: 2, then 3, 4, 5, 6,
  7 and 8, up to a 9-fold zig-zag mesh in the last, fastest A. On the break chord the mesh snaps open to one slow wave.
- **Register as space.** The floor in statement 1, then one storey up for the violas, then the ceiling when the violins
  enter, then the whole vault.
- **Flee** on the strings' mass in the frenzy. **The casket** saved for a single slash on the final chord.

**Serendipity:**
- **The weave as a tempo gauge (critic).** Strings' `crossings` is set per note, so the accelerando tightens the weave one
  step per section, into a jittery 9-fold zig-zag. On the break chord it snaps open to a single slow wave: stillness you
  can see.
- **The downbeat blowout (composer).** It was found by accident. The push's wall of chord bars and the first cymbal (one
  huge tear that hangs for 2.5 s) burst into white-hot slabs as the ff lands. The critic held the wall's colour below
  white until that moment.
- **The mezzanine (critic).** When the tune turns to F# major, a second copy of the threads lights one storey above the cave.
- **Held notes as dread (critic).** The bassoons' squares are bigger on held notes. The first long note of the piece lands
  as one big slab.
- **The knot (composer).** Woven threads cast against type: a closed ring for the diminished chord.
- **The casket held back for 149 s** for one slash on the final chord.

**Bent rules, stated plainly:**
- The cymbals and crashes come from the audio. Their pitches are nominal (B-minor chord tones), because blocks places its

- Statement 1 is spare for 40 s, below the screen-full rule, on purpose: it is the pp cave.
- Blocks' inverted bands flash cyan at the downbeat, the break crash and the coda crashes. That is the widget's own look.
