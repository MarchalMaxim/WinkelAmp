# Romeo and Juliet, 5. The return: B "Ink and wash"

- **Piece:** Tchaikovsky, *Romeo and Juliet* fantasy-overture: the love theme's return, the feud, the catastrophe and the last blows (0:00-3:41).
- **Show:** `romeo-juliet-5-return-b-ink-and-wash` (built by `build-romeo-juliet-5-return-b-ink-and-wash.mjs`).
- **Keywords:** the love theme, feuding families, fate, the tragic ending.
- **Palette:** vermilion and cobalt liquids over a grey night sea; then graphite and red-scratched pens, bone bars and gold-rimmed soot ink on black; blood bars at the great blows; at the end, bone ink over a colourless sea.

## What to judge

- **Duet's harmony rule as the plot (untested).** The two liquids lean together and lie against each other on consonant chords. On dissonant ones they are flung apart and spatter. What they hear is chosen to tell the story: in the love music they hear the theme and its bass. In the feud they hear only its harsh chords whole; the other accents reach them as single strokes. In the love music they mostly lie together (66 consonant attacks to 26 dissonant). In the second half of the catastrophe they are mostly torn apart (28 to 47).
- **Horizon as the lovers' night sea, with Duet's reflection on it (untested).** A second Duet clip draws the same lovers as a small, soft copy turned upside down low in the sea, so their colours mirror on the water. It is faint. Does it read as a reflection?
- **Brush on the sighs:** one bone stroke for each oboe or clarinet sigh, then for the love theme's long leaning notes.
- **Scribble as a growing thicket.** In the feud's return, each line stays longer the later it is drawn (7 s, rising to 16 s), so the pens fill the frame as the feud builds.
- **Kline bone bars, then blood bars.** A bone bar for each sword clash. Blood red from the first of the three great blows (3:15.6).
- **Rorschach's fate, as one arc:** small omens under the broken theme and the feud's bass, then gold-rimmed soot blots on black that grow wave by wave through the catastrophe, then a flood at the three great blows, then bone.

**Serendipity:** Duet's own physics carries the story: the lovers lie together or spring apart because of the harmony itself, and the feud is filtered so that they hear only its dissonances (the composer's find). Their reflection is a second Duet in an upside-down small copy. The love music's sea comes back at the very end, drained of colour, under fate's bone blot. Scribble's line life grows into a thicket. The critic found that the brown mud blamed on fate's ink was in fact Kline's own night ground leaking through the see-through blend. With blend `lighten` the catastrophe is black, and fate's soot reads as a gold-rimmed flower that grows wave by wave instead of a veil. The critic also gave fate small omens under the feud's return, so its thread never breaks between the broken theme and the catastrophe. The same blend lets the blood bars stain the cobalt lover violet where they cross.

Notes: Rorschach is random, so the final bone shape changes from render to render (a moth, two lobes, a star). The first blood bar now lands on the first great blow. Kline ranks loudness within its own small set, so the blood set needed two notes per blow and `thresh` 0.5. A few love-theme notes are transcribed a semitone off, so a few flinches answer tuning errors rather than real chromatic notes.
