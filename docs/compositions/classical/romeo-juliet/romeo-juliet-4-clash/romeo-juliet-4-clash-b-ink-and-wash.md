# Romeo and Juliet, 4. The clash: B "Ink and wash"

- **Piece:** Tchaikovsky, *Romeo and Juliet* fantasy-overture, the development (0:00-2:40).
- **Show:** `romeo-juliet-4-clash-b-ink-and-wash`
- **Keywords:** feuding families, the Friar Lawrence chorale, fate, clashing swords.
- **Palette:** a gold web cut by bone bars and red-scratched pens, on black, around a lit rose window of umber glass. Sepia ink rises out of it.

## What to judge

- **Klee as a rose window (new).** The strings' whole mosaic is stretched into a soft oval in the middle of the frame: Friar Lawrence's cell window, its tiles drawn out into narrow panes. The oval's soft edge fades the corners, so the lowest and highest notes show only toward the middle. Fifths' web is strung across the glass, and the pens, bars and ink work on black around it. Before this change Klee covered the whole frame in brown.
- **Fifths and Scribble crossing at ff:** the Friar's hymn against the feud. This combination is untested.
- **Kline's bone bars from the audio:** the sword strokes, in a full-frame window with blend `lighten` (a region setting) so the bars stay solid and leave no grey veil. Its faint night ground shows on the black, so each Kline clip starts just as a stroke lands.
- **Rorschach soot ink with its gold rim (ink 2), from omens to a flood.** The soot is dark, so it shows only where it stains the glass. Outside the window only its gold tentacles show.
- **Pixel sort with no colour of its own (`own` 0) on the real ff blows only:** it drags the paint under it.
- **Coverage:** only 24 % of the notes are transcribed. The trumpets, the hammered F# and the clashes come from the audio (chroma and onsets).

**Serendipity:**
- (Critic) Klee is cast against type: the full-frame ground becomes a small copy stretched into a rose window. The story became "confined to the cell, then bursting out". The web breaks out at the climax, and the ink floods the frame on the last F#.
- (Critic) Rorschach's soot shows only on the glass, its gold rim everywhere.
- (Critic) On the hammers Kline goes silent: the swords vanish with the web, and one bar returns on the last F#.
- (Composer) The trumpets' chorale is rebuilt from the audio chroma and drawn larger than the picture.
- (Composer) Pixel sort only drags the wet paint (`own` 0) and adds no colour.
