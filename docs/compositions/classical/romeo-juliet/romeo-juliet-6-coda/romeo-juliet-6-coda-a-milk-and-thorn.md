# Romeo and Juliet 6, Coda: A "Milk and thorn"

**Piece:** Prokofiev, *Romeo and Juliet*: the coda (funeral march, Friar Lawrence's chorale, the love theme transfigured, the hammered closing chords).
**Concept:** the cycle's last page, told with the same marks the cycle used throughout, each one at its end. Fate is red snap squares, the love theme is Ivory rows, the friar's chorale is milky handover slabs, and the ground is a field.
**Keywords:** funeral march timpani · love theme broken · Friar Lawrence chorale · tragic ending.
**Palette:** heavy red squares and ageing keys on black; the last milk slabs on plum; at the end, oxblood.

## What to judge

- **Fold (not judged yet):** a still, upright mirror on the transfigured love (1:42-2:21). Twin rows of keys face each other across a gold seam.
- **Red snap squares over milky handover in a ff climax (new, and a deliberate risk):** in nocturne a2 you called red squares beside milky handover "the incongruent red". Here red is set against milk on purpose, because that contrast is the show's title. The nearest liked case is nocturne b3, with handover on a slow line and small red squares. Does it read as one family here, or as incongruent again?
- **A field that seeps in:** it starts as a dim under-glow and darkens chord by chord until the last blow, where it floods oxblood.
- **Ivory as a broken theme:** cracked rows (cracks 5) in small copies, each lower on the screen than the last.
- **Handover alone as a frame:** the chorale returns the way the friar's chorale opened the cycle. It is spare on purpose; is 53 s too long?

**Serendipity:**
- **Composer:** the love theme transfigured as a mirror. Fold comes back from the feud, where it meant hatred, now as the two houses reconciled. Found in a preview: as the mirror fades, the twin dies a moment before the original, just as fate's roll begins.
- **Critic:** the title made literal at the close. The hammered final chords are B major (the consolation in the major key). They take the friar's milk, so for the first time milk and thorn share the screen. The oxblood seeps in under them instead of switching on at the end.

**Still weak:**
- The march (0:08-0:47) is thin: one square at a time and small rows, 2-3 threads, about 40 s long.
- The chorale is spare: two slabs over plum.
- The tutti stair is the same shape six times, because the music hammers the same chord.
