# Romeo and Juliet, 3. Love theme, B: "Ink and wash"

**Piece:** Tchaikovsky, *Romeo and Juliet* fantasy overture, the love-theme section (pack `romeo-juliet-3-love`, 3:41).
Show `romeo-juliet-3-love-b-ink-and-wash`. Open it with `/?show=romeo-juliet-3-love-b-ink-and-wash`.

**Concept:** the lovers are Duet's two liquids. The love theme moves the quick cobalt drop and the bass under it moves
the thick vermilion one. On consonant chords they lean together along a soft seam; on dissonant ones they are thrown
apart. The sighs are written in bone ink on a hanging scroll, the harp blooms a mandala halo, the horns lay a gold wreath
over the pair, and the balcony night is a grey Sugimoto sea whose horizon sinks with each modulation.

**Keywords:** star-crossed lovers, love theme, sighing strings, balcony night.
**Palette:** vermilion and cobalt liquids over a grey night sea, bone ink, gold lace, one red ensō.

## What to judge

- **Duet as lead (its first show).** Do two liquids judged by the harmony read as two lovers? Watch the climax: they
  press together, and the theme's clashing appoggiaturas over the A-flat pedal make them flinch but not part.
- **Duet with Horizon** (untested pair): flat, vivid colour over a near-monochrome photographic sea.
- **Calligraphy as a hanging scroll** (new use of a region): a small copy of the brush turned 90 degrees into a tall
  strip at the right. The phrases become top-to-bottom columns, and the dried ink stays as the letter's earlier lines.
- **Two mandalas with two jobs:** the harp's halo behind the lovers (0:39, 3:07) and the horns' gold wreath laid over
  them (1:31-2:50).
- **Spare start:** 20 seconds of almost black sea before anything moves. Is that stillness too long?

**Serendipity:**
- The scroll. Written across the sky, the sighs left grey scribbles that never dried away. Turned into a scroll, the same
  residue becomes a letter that fills up over the scene.
- The horizon sinks a step at each modulation (G, E, A), so the lovers rise out of the sea into an opening sky (the
  composer's idea).
- One red ensō, drawn once only, on the loudest beat (2:12.4).
- An accident we kept: in the balcony night the physics leaves the vermilion in the sky and the cobalt down in the sea,
  one above the other like the balcony and the garden.
