# Liszt: Hungarian Rhapsody No. 2 (orchestral), show A: "Bandleader's octaves"

`shows/liszt-hungarian-2-a-bandleaders-octaves.json`. Open it as `/?show=liszt-hungarian-2-a-bandleaders-octaves`. Built by `build-liszt-hungarian-2-a-bandleaders-octaves.mjs` in this folder.

- **Concept.** The loved Octave storm stack (nocturne-micro-a5) is grown into a 10½-minute rhapsody. Every clip listens to its own pick set: the notes one hears as that strand.
- **Keywords.** Brooding lassan, cimbalom tremolo, virtuoso fireworks, csardas dance.
- **Palette.** Bone needles and milk-white keys, ember-gold sparks and oxblood bursts, on black over a dim violet field.

## What to judge

- **Does the Octave storm stack carry a whole arc?** The stack is Interlock needles, Hammer bars, Pluck threads, Fireworks-snap and fields. Here it runs 10 minutes, not 7 seconds.
  - Is the slow lassan (0:00-6:03) full enough?
  - Is the friska (6:03-10:31) too full?
- **Gliss in motion on real runs.** Until now it was only seen in stills. Watch both clarinet cadenzas, the glitter, the whirl and the spray.
- **Pluck cast as a cimbalom.** Its tremolos and knocks become fans of hairlines.
- **Ivory on violin sequences** instead of a piano line. It plays one short row per phrase, and also in small windows for the friska dawn.
- **Fireworks held back.** Not one burst in the first 6:50. The first goes off at 6:50.7, the first friska tutti.
- **Interlock as solid wedges.** Its needles play inside a full-screen window, so they cut solid oxblood wedges into the lit field. This is new; see Serendipity.

**Serendipity:**
- **Solid wedges** (found by the critic in the preview). Interlock paints with "lighten", so over a lit field its dark red needles vanished. Only the white strikes showed, and the brighter the music, the less you saw. Played in a full-screen window, the needles draw alone on black, and the window keeps anything lit solid. Now they cut solid oxblood wedges into the glowing field at any brightness.
- **Blaze, then black.** The lassan's ff and its climax raise the field and the thicket of wedges to their fullest at the very instant the orchestra breaks off. The silence is the event.
- **The dip's whisper in octaves** (also the critic's find). The violin lane hears the murmured tune running in octaves, so it is drawn as paired bars: the title's octaves at pp.
- **From the composer:**
  - a close-up window on the cellos' low register;
  - the dawn's tiny Ivory rows walking closer;
  - a gliss turned 180 degrees for the plunge;
  - fireworks withheld for 6:50;
  - the final "held chord" heard as two blows, from the audio.
