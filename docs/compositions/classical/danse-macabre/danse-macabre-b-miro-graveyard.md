# Danse macabre · B · Miró graveyard

**Piece:** Saint-Saëns, *Danse macabre* (7:10). **Show:** `/?show=danse-macabre-b-miro-graveyard`
**Concept:** Miró and Rorschach, folded on the tritone. The danse is a Miró constellation on a cream sheet: the waltz is its one black line, and its held notes swell into red, blue and yellow blobs. The pizzicato bass is a sepia Rorschach blot under the stars. Death's fiddle scrawls in holes of night torn in the sheet. In the Dies irae and the frenzy, a fold mirrors the whole page.
**Keywords:** ghostly waltz, Death's fiddle, the devil's tritone, Dies irae.
**Palette:** cream sheet, one black line, primary-colour blobs, sepia ink, bone and red pen in the night.

## What to judge

- **Miró + Rorschach (untested combo).** Do Miró's line and blobs and the sepia blot read as one family on one sheet, or as two pictures stacked?
- **Scribble as holes of night (new use).** Scribble is invisible on cream, so it is drawn in a window that keeps its black: wherever Death plays, the sheet tears open. Is that a theme, or a gimmick?
- **The fugato's four pits (2:00-2:30).** Instead of one hole, four small holes open one per entering voice, until the sheet is perforated. This is the critic's main change.
- **Fold on a cream ground.** See the ghost mirror at the tuning, the Ab-octave swing (2:50), the Dies irae and the frenzy kaleidoscope.
- **Rorschach cast against type.** In the chorale it is a slow indigo rose window (sym 6, ink 1), not a violent blot.

**Serendipity:**
- **The composer's find.** Scribble cannot draw on cream, so it became a hole of night torn in Miró's sheet that widens each time Death plays.
- **The composer's other choices.** Rorschach is cast against type as an indigo rose in the Dies irae, and the fold's folds multiply on each ff blow of the frenzy.
- **The critic's change in the fugato.** Four instances of scribble in four places, one per voice, assigned by entry and register (the transcription bleeds between the string tracks). The fugue is shown as a sheet perforated voice by voice.
- **The critic's change in the recap.** The recap's night sits above the ink, so it is clean. The fold creeps in on a rising intensity curve before the frenzy, so the frenzy's kaleidoscope has a prologue and its night bursts in on a bright sheet.

**Knobs moved from our defaults:**
- **miro:** `life` and `swell` in the chorale.
- **rorschach:** `size` (0.7 and 0.85), `sym` (2 for the tolls, 6 for the rose) and `ink` 1 for the rose.
- **scribble:** `pens`, `weight` and `stay`, for the small windows and the lone pen.
- **fold:** `strength`, `flip`, `seam` and `turn` per scene, plus `folds` per note in the frenzy.
