# Barber, Adagio for Strings · A · Milk and sea

**Piece:** Samuel Barber, Adagio for Strings (about 7:46). One long climb, a cut, then an epilogue.
**Concept:** the proven family pushed further. Milky handover and huddled Ivory sit over a still Sugimoto sea, and the violas and cellos are a murmuration.
**Keywords it embodies:** slow ascent, searing climax, silence, hymn-like.
**Palette:** a grey long-exposure sea with milky slabs, white keys and a pale flock on it. The flock turns dark red whenever it sinks into its knot (held notes, gaps), rose at the untranscribed swell, and blood red at the stretto and the climax. At the climax, grey and ember sorting.
**Show:** `shows/barber-adagio-a-milk-and-sea.json` (built by `build-barber-adagio-a-milk-and-sea.mjs`). Preview: `renders/preview/preview-barber-adagio-a-milk-and-sea.mp4`.

## What to judge

- **Horizon as a ground that tells the whole arc.** This is untested as a full-length ground. The how-present curve and the knob values carried on its own notes make it change with the music. It starts as a hairline and turns to fog in the chant. It rises and hardens with each swell. At the climax it is a knife edge with a white sky over a black sea. It is cut dead, reborn at the reprise, and ends as a hairline.
- **Horizon + murmuration + pixel sort on a real climax** (untested). Each fff chord re-forms the flock into that chord's figure, and pixel sort pours the keys and the sea into grey and ember sheets.
- **Handover on its ideal material.** Barber's long, slow notes get one milky slab each, gone as the next lands. Judge whether the slabs read as milk or as grey-khaki boxes; at low velocity they are greyish.
- **Rising is built, falling is handed over.** Every rising phrase is a huddled Ivory row standing on the horizon line, each row larger than the last. Falling and winding phrases are milk slabs.
- **The flock's red.** The knot's dark red is the widget's fixed dread colour, and no knob turns it off. It appears whenever the flock sinks into its knot on a held note or in a gap: on purpose in the dark middle (about 220-228 s), and still briefly at a few quiet moments (about 0:30, 3:10, 6:38). The swell figures are deliberately rose, not blood (bleed 0.2), and the full red is saved for the stretto and the climax. Judge whether the knot in the quiet passages bothers you.

**Serendipity:**
- **Composer:** the summit row's crater (Ivory crater 1) stands behind the keys like a dark island on the horizon. It was kept, and the keys stand on through the whole fff while each chord sorts them into the sea.
- **Critic:** the reprise's octave doubling is drawn as each Ivory row turned over in the water, a second dim instance of the same widget on the same notes, rotated 180 degrees. Because of the turn it hangs to the side, like a playing card, not straight under the row.
- **Critic:** the strings' untranscribed swell is drawn as the flock's chord figures, a first sight of the device the climax uses.

**Not done, worth a look:** pale chord figures of the flock under the hymn were considered and left out. The hymn's soft chords would not all pass the widget's bar, so some chords would get no mark.
