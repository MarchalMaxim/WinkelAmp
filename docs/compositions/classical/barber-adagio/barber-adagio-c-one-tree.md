# Barber, Adagio for Strings · C · One tree

- **Show:** `barber-adagio-c-one-tree` (open as `/?show=barber-adagio-c-one-tree`). Built by `build-barber-adagio-c-one-tree.mjs` in this folder.
- **Concept:** one Tree as the only hero. Its built-in arc (it grows, a climax, then a blight) is the Adagio's own arc.
- **Keywords:** slow ascent, searing climax, grief, silence.
- **Palette:** ginkgo gold leaves on a grey-blue sky that turns to violet dusk. The hymn is in first frost: white leaves on a cold blue.

## What to judge

- **Tree as a sole hero, for eight minutes.** This casting breaks the "screen full, four threads" rule on purpose. One tree hears every voice: violin, viola and cello lines, the bass, the cadence and hymn chords, plus notes measured from the audio where the transcription is silent. Does one widget hold a whole piece?
- **Two copies of the same tree, crossfaded (new).**
  - A thin lace (`leaves` 0.25, a knob moved for the concept) carries the opening and the middle.
  - The full tree (our default) is let in only on the swells' pressure points. Each swell leaves the tree fuller than the last, it thins again in the low strophe, and it is whole for the first time on the climax's first chord.
- **Tree's own climax detection.** Its blight starts at 5:28, on the climax's first chord, with no knob set. The leaves then turn and fall through the hymn and the reprise.
- **The casket on Tree (untested pairing).** It cuts the golden crown on the climax's chord changes only, with drips set to 0.

**Serendipity:**
- The iris. The composer's opening shows the tree only through a soft halo that dawns with the theme. It reads as light behind the trunk.
- The frost twin. The hymn's remote chords get the same tree in another season.
- The critic's two-tree crossfade. A thin and a full copy of one tree, on one pick set, let the canopy follow Barber's three rising swells instead of filling up by 2:30.
  - Their big limbs coincide, because the layout comes from the notes.
  - Their fine twigs differ a little. While the crown is half in, this shows as a faint second lace, like depth or mist. That was not planned, and it is kept.

**Known weak spots:**
- The halo bands a little at preview quality.
- 2:30-4:15 is a calm, lacy picture with little change. That suits the music, but it is long.
