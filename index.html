Playing with WinkelAmp

With the mouse

  • Click — splash color
  • Drag — paint with the sound
  • Scroll — zoom in and out
  • Drop files — play your music

With the keyboard

  • Space play / pause
  • 1–4 scenes
  • R surprise me
  • M next mirror
  • H hide the interface
  • F fullscreen
  • ← → previous / next track

How sound becomes color

Every note has a color. Notes that sound good together sit next to each other on the color wheel (it follows the circle of fifths), so a song keeps its own palette and a key change shifts the mood. Drums and noise turn white and sparkly; loud means bright. Open Sound → Motion to decide which part of the sound drives size, speed, spin and more.
