level: jumps on an attack, halves every 0.8 s loud: the loudness lane, read 1.1 s ahead, stretched to this piece pace: attacks a second around now, against this piece's busiest

What to look for. In a swell or a held chord the left picture shrinks back between attacks while the middle one stays big as long as the sound does. In a fast run the right one grows, however soft the notes are. In the Hall of the Mountain King shows all three: it gets louder and faster at once.

Keys: Space plays and pauses, ← and → move five seconds. The address keeps the song, widget and knob (?song=…&widget=…&knob=…).
