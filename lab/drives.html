Loud, rel and pace drives

One widget four times over the same recording, with the same knob on: level (louder notes), loud (the recording's loudness, quiet end to loud end of the whole piece), rel (louder than around it: the same sound judged against the seconds before and after) and pace (how busy the music is). Each knob runs from its lowest value at 0 to its highest at 1.

level: jumps on an attack, halves every 0.8 s loud: no lag (0.4 s window centred on now, stretched to this piece) rel: the same, against the 8 s on either side, half against the whole piece pace: attacks a second around now, against this piece's busiest

What to look for. In a swell or a held chord the level picture shrinks back between attacks while loud and rel stay big as long as the sound does. In a quiet stretch loud sits near its lowest, while rel still swells with each rise; at the real climax both are at the top, and rel sinks back sooner once a loud stretch goes on. In the Barber, compare the quiet opening with the climax before the cut at 5:53. In a fast run pace grows, however soft the notes are. In the Hall of the Mountain King all of them rise at once.
