How to read it. The grey shape is all a widget knows about how loud the music is: it jumps on each transcribed attack and halves every 0.8 s. A held chord, a swell on one bow or a passage the transcriber missed drains it to nothing while the sound itself stays loud or grows. The blue line is the sound's energy, measured the way the ear weighs it (K-weighting, as in broadcast loudness meters) over a 3 s window centred on now, which only an offline tool can do, so it has no lag. The green line is what a one-click "follows the sound" preset would write into an intensity lane: the energy climbing fast and falling slowly, stretched to this piece's own range, never below 0.35 so the screen does not empty.

Keys: Space plays and pauses, ← and → move five seconds.
