A knob can stay fixed, or follow one of five things the music does: louder = the keys are struck harder · faster = more notes per second · higher notes = the top note climbs · more bass = more weight in the low notes · fuller chords = more notes sounding at once. “louder → more” turns the knob up as the music gets louder; “louder → less” turns it down. The two small pictures at the ends of a slider show what left and right do; the lit one by its name shows where it is now, moving with the music. Hover or tap i for a sentence about it.
