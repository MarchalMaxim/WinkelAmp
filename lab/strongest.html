top row: a random selection (today) bottom row: the strongest notes left out

What to look for. At a low intensity the right picture answers only the notes you would point at: the accents, the loudest chord of a phrase, the hits of a build. The left one reacts to any note, loud or soft, so it flickers along without a shape. Move the slider up and watch the right side fill in from the strongest notes down, while the left side only grows denser. At 100 % both hear every note; at 0 % neither hears any. On a track with notes all equally loud the right side still picks at random, spread evenly, but not the same notes as the left. In the Hall of the Mountain King, compare the quiet opening with the loud end.

Keys: Space plays and pauses, ← and → move five seconds. The address keeps the song, widget and intensity (?song=…&widget=…&a=0.3).
